School

School Event Portal

Create events, generate/download attendance QR codes, and complete post-event information for your School.

1. Create New Event

Complete the CHEDS Pre-Event Form. The system generates an Event ID and Attendance QR.

2. Find My Event

Use Event ID and creator CUD email to retrieve your event and complete the post-event submission.

3. Attendance QR

Participants scan the downloaded QR to open the attendance sheet. QR attendance is counted automatically.

Post-event lock: After the organizer submits Manual Attendance and Evidence, the record is locked. The organizer can view it but cannot modify or submit it again. Only IRP can edit/unlock it.
